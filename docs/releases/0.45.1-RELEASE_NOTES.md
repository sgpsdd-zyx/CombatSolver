# CombatSolver 0.45.1 (multiplayer fork)

## 简体中文

相对多人 fork 0.44.1，本版合入官方 0.45.0，并带来持续伤害的多人选路改进。

- 中毒等游戏未标明来源的实际扣血，现在按本阶段人数折算，参与持续伤害与防御的权衡。建议摘要单独显示这部分价值，不把它记作你的个人贡献。
- 感谢 [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012) 提交的 [PR #126](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/126) 和 [PR #128](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/128)：第三方玩法模组可以适配回合开始前及玩家抽牌后的效果。已适配效果按游戏顺序参与计算；多人仍保留死亡队友的效果停用和队友选牌边界，相关模组需要登记具体效果。
- 感谢 [xhj66](https://github.com/xhj66) 提交的 [PR #127](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/127)：修复击杀后仍预测给已离场敌人施加减益，以及能力或遗物触发的效果被误算成当前卡牌施加的问题，减少中毒和遗物触发与实战不一致的情况。
- 与 Loadout v0.5.6 同时使用且没有配置额外怪物能力时，不再误报其召唤监听器不兼容。配置了怪物能力或使用其他未经验证版本时，仍会明确停止计算。
- 感谢 [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012) 提交的 [PR #130](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/130)：已查阅世界线数量、已用时间、内存条和搜索进度平滑更新，问题包上传进度更平顺；单人自动执行时，当前动作高亮随出牌节奏变化。
- 求解器窗口记住拖动后的位置；下场战斗、重启游戏及窗口临时缩小后恢复尺寸时，继续使用保存的位置。
- 多人仍由本机手动重新计算和操作，最多推演十四个敌方周期。队友独立决策，无需服从或等待建议；军师不预测其未来主动操作。建议可能改善，也可能恶化具体战斗结果，尚未验证真实联机胜率与可见性能。单人沿用官方 0.45.0 的策略与参数。

## English

Compared with multiplayer fork 0.44.1, this release incorporates upstream 0.45.0 and improves how multiplayer advice values damage over time.

- Actual HP damage without a source identified by the game, including Poison, now contributes to the damage-over-time versus defense tradeoff after division among the current stage's participants. The advice summary displays this value separately; it does not count as your personal contribution.
- [PR #126](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/126) and [PR #128](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/128) by [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012): Gameplay mods can provide adapters for effects before a side's turn starts and after the player draws at turn start. Adapted effects run in game order. Multiplayer preserves inactive effects on dead teammates and stops at choices requiring a teammate; each mod still needs to register its effects.
- [PR #127](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/127) by [xhj66](https://github.com/xhj66): Fixed predictions that applied debuffs to enemies already removed by a killing blow, and effects from Powers or relics being mistaken for effects applied by the current card. This reduces mismatches in Poison and relic triggers.
- Fixed an erroneous incompatibility report with Loadout v0.5.6 when no extra monster Powers are configured. Configured monster Powers and other unverified Loadout versions still stop calculation with an explicit explanation.
- [PR #130](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/130) by [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012): Reviewed-worldline counts, elapsed time, memory bars, search progress and report uploads now update smoothly. During single-player auto-play, action highlights follow the pace of the route.
- The solver overlay remembers its dragged position across combats and game restarts, and restores that position after a temporarily smaller window is enlarged again.
- Multiplayer still requires the local player to request recalculation and play manually, with a limit of fourteen enemy cycles. Teammates make their own decisions and need not follow or wait for advice; their future active actions are not predicted. Advice can improve or worsen individual outcomes. Live multiplayer win rates and visible performance remain unverified. Single-player retains upstream 0.45.0 strategies and settings.
