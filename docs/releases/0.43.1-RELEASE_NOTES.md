# CombatSolver 0.43.1 (fork)

本 fork 汇总自 0.41.3 以来的变化，合入官方 0.42.0 与 0.43.0。感谢上游作者及贡献者；原版更新说明见 [0.42.0](0.42.0-RELEASE_NOTES.md) 和 [0.43.0](0.43.0-RELEASE_NOTES.md)。

## 简体中文

- 单人模式的全自动与自动计算可以分别控制。关闭自动计算后，已算好的路线可以保留；开启全自动时仍能继续执行有效路线。回合开始按计划手动选牌后可继续原路线，选择不同的牌则按实际战况处理。
- 单人新增“冻结路线”，展开面板和小窗均可使用。冻结后暂停自动重算与全自动接管；战况变化后，旧路线仅供参考，不能采用或执行。点击“重新计算”解除冻结。小窗也可直接采用当前候选路线。
- 改善候选路线中的回合开始选牌预览、紧凑战损与回血摘要，以及药水、成长、遗物面板的收起操作。回血不会抵消累计扣血，也不会仅因跨回合就丢失已发生的回血。
- 单人新增“预知战后药水奖励”，默认关闭。开启后可显示掉落预估，并在药水栏已满时用于智能用药取舍。多人不预读战后奖励，已用药水按本机玩家统计；修改这个单人设置不会清掉当前多人建议。
- 合入金斧、电流相生、扯碎、亡魂牵引、谋杀和超质量体的整场历史状态区分修复。该修复覆盖搜索开始时已在相关牌堆中的牌；之后生成、或根时已消耗再回收的同类牌仍不在其范围内。
- 合入重复生成卡牌查询、路线续用信息与低内存搜索处理改进。特别长的搜索会限制保留的历史状态记录，触及限制后仍继续搜索，但最终路线可能与旧版不同。

多人继续采用手动计算、手动操作、最多七个敌方周期和每周期 3 HP 本机扣血目标；保留 0.41.3 的胜利判断、早停和本机用药修复。多人面板在展开与收起时均不提供自动执行、采用或冻结操作。默认策略和风险顺序没有加入新的研究实验。

本版未完成真实联机、可见游戏性能或所有战斗的质量验收，不承诺每场战斗都更强或更快。

## English

This fork incorporates upstream 0.42.0 and 0.43.0, with changes since fork 0.41.3.

- Single-player auto-play and automatic calculation can be controlled separately. Disabling automatic calculation preserves an existing route, while auto-play can continue a valid route. Making the planned turn-start choice manually can reuse that route; a different choice is handled against the actual combat state.
- Added single-player "Freeze route" in both expanded and compact views. It pauses automatic recalculation and auto-play takeover. An outdated frozen route stays visible for reference but cannot be adopted or executed. "Recalculate" unfreezes it. The compact view can also adopt the current candidate.
- Improved turn-start choice previews, compact damage and healing summaries, and collapse controls for potion, growth and relic panels. Healing does not cancel cumulative damage, and healing already received remains included after a turn transition.
- Added single-player "Foresee post-combat potion reward", off by default. When enabled, the forecast informs Smart potion use with a full belt. Multiplayer does not read future rewards and counts used potions for the local player only. Changing this single-player preference preserves current multiplayer advice.
- Incorporated combat-history state distinctions for Gold Axe, Voltaic, Tear Asunder, Pull From Below, Murder and Supermassive. This covers cards in the relevant piles at the search root; cards generated later, or already Exhausted at the root and recovered later, remain outside its scope.
- Incorporated improvements to repeated card-catalog queries, route continuation data and low-memory handling. Exceptionally long searches cap retained history records. Search continues after the cap, but the selected route may differ from older versions.

Multiplayer remains manual, with up to seven enemy cycles and a local damage target of 3 HP per cycle. The victory, early-stop and local potion fixes from 0.41.3 remain in place. Both panel sizes hide execution, adoption and freeze controls in multiplayer. No new research experiment changes the default multiplayer strategy or risk ordering.

Live multiplayer, visible-game performance and quality across all encounters have not been validated. This release does not promise a stronger or faster result in every fight.
