# CombatSolver 0.50.1

## 简体中文

多人 fork 更新，合入官方 0.50.0。以下为相对本 fork 0.49.2 的变化。

- 感谢 [洛天琉璃依（ltlly）](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #207](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/207)：单人模式减少复杂战斗中的重复计算，并改善相同战损下的省药选择。部分优化仅在官方已支持的运行环境和战斗条件下启用。
- 感谢 [Fzhenhong](https://github.com/Fzhenhong) 提交的 [PR #211](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/211)：改善单人智能药水选择和复杂战斗的继续搜索，让能明显降低战损的用药路线更容易被找到。
- 感谢 [fdkgf](https://github.com/fdkgf) 提交的 [PR #215](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/215)：单人模式找到胜利路线后减少重复搜索；同等战损和资源收益下，可能保留回合数较多的胜利路线。
- 单人智能药水在开局收益不足时减少无效等待；考虑战后换药机会，同时保留明显省血的用药路线。
- 感谢 [bingyang1132](https://github.com/bingyang1132) 提交的 [PR #217](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/217)：完善内容 Mod 的适配支持，涵盖额外回合、新获得的状态效果和招式被修改的原版怪物；需要对应适配器支持。
- 改善第三方 Mod 兼容性识别，减少框架及商店类 Mod 的不兼容误报。遇到尚未适配的战斗内容时显示所属 Mod 并停止计算。
- 改善问题反馈提示：手动操作使结果过期，或本场包含第三方内容时，不再提示上传问题包；普通计算故障仍保留诊断提示。
- 修复部分内容 Mod 的效果预加载导致计算启动失败，以及部分移动运行环境下内存管理不兼容的问题。
- 修复虚空形态强制结束回合后，知识恶魔及下一回合的选牌可能执行失败的问题。
- 修复群体减益触发凶恶、地狱狂徒并击杀敌人后，后续结算可能导致计算失败的问题。
- 修复玩家死亡后的金币结算，以及实战继续推进后路线能量、抽牌数预测不准确的问题。
- 修复回合准备时漏算延迟能量、镀金缆线与等离子的额外能量，以及忍术卷轴首回合生成的小刀。
- 修复佩尔之眼与彼岸咆哮同时存在时，回合末自动出牌和额外回合的结算顺序。多人继续按各自持有者处理额外回合。
- 修复瀑布巨兽改变行动后，眼部攻击的虚弱效果预测错误。
- 修复铁斩波、武装和挑衅的格挡被反伤消耗后重复计算，造成战损预测偏低的问题。
- 修复跨回合自动执行时，旧回合进度覆盖新路线并引发界面错误的问题。
- 多人继续使用本机贡献目标和手动重算，支持烘焙手套选牌期间计算；本次单人搜索优化不改变多人选路规则。

已知限制：部分缩小甲虫战斗的启动失败仍待排查。实际联机表现、可见性能及移动端运行未在本 fork 本轮验收；不承诺特定提速倍数。

## English

Multiplayer fork update incorporating upstream 0.50.0. Changes since this fork's 0.49.2:

- [PR #207](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/207) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Reduces repeated work in complex single-player battles and improves potion conservation at equal HP loss. Some optimizations apply only in officially supported runtime environments and combat conditions.
- [PR #211](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/211) by [Fzhenhong](https://github.com/Fzhenhong): Improves single-player Smart potion choices and continued searching in complex battles, making routes that save substantial HP easier to find.
- [PR #215](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/215) by [fdkgf](https://github.com/fdkgf): Reduces repeated single-player searching after finding a victory. With equal HP loss and resource rewards, a winning route that takes more turns may be retained.
- Single-player Smart potion searches avoid unnecessary opening searches when the benefit is too small, while accounting for post-combat replacement opportunities and retaining useful HP-saving routes.
- [PR #217](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/217) by [bingyang1132](https://github.com/bingyang1132): Expands content mod adapter support for extra turns, newly gained status effects, and vanilla monsters with modified moves. Requires support from the corresponding adapter.
- Improves third-party mod detection and reduces false incompatibility reports for framework and shop mods. Unsupported combat content identifies its mod and stops calculation.
- Improves report prompts: results made stale by manual input and combats involving third-party content no longer prompt players to upload a report. Ordinary calculation failures retain diagnostic guidance.
- Fixes calculation startup failures caused by preloading effects from some content mods, and incompatible memory management on some mobile runtimes.
- Fixes card-choice execution involving Knowledge Demon and the next player turn after Void Form forces the turn to end.
- Fixes calculation failures when area debuffs trigger Vicious and Hellraiser, kill an enemy, and continue resolving effects.
- Fixes gold settlement after a player's death and route energy or draw predictions being affected by the live battle advancing.
- Fixes missing delayed energy during turn setup, extra energy from Gold-Plated Cables with Plasma, and first-turn Shivs from Ninja Scroll.
- Fixes end-of-turn automatic play and extra-turn ordering with Pael's Eye and Howl from Beyond. Multiplayer continues to resolve extra turns for their individual owners.
- Fixes Go for the Eyes predicting Weak incorrectly after Waterfall Giant changes its move.
- Fixes block from Iron Wave, Armaments, and Taunt being counted twice after retaliation damage consumes it, causing predicted HP loss to be too low.
- Fixes progress from the previous turn overwriting a new route and causing interface errors during automatic execution.
- Multiplayer retains local contribution goals, manual recalculation, and calculation during Toasty Mittens choices. These single-player search optimizations do not change multiplayer route selection.

Known limits: startup failures in some Shrinker Beetle battles remain under investigation. Live multiplayer, visible performance, and mobile execution were not validated in this fork's current release work; no specific speedup is promised.
