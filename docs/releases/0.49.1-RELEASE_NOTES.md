# CombatSolver 0.49.1（多人 fork）

## 简体中文

相对于本 fork 0.48.1 的更新，包含官方 0.48.1 至 0.49.0 的改进：

- 多人同步本次金币、生命上限及金纸结算改进，分别处理本机和队友的遗物、死亡与复活状态。
- 修正手动喝药后的“更优世界线”误判：每额外使用一瓶药，预计整场战损至少下降 9 HP 才会提示；原路线已经计划使用的药不会重复计入成本。
- 问题反馈与日志增加额外用药数量和扣除药水成本后的战损改善量；旧报告缺少用药信息时标为未知。
- 修复倾泻配合不休陀螺时的抽牌时机预测错误，减少选牌错误和路线执行中止。
- 修复金纸配合生成牌时，回合末耗尽的预测与实际结果不一致的问题。
- 修复压缩生成燃料时的预测手牌顺序，以及子弹时间后部分卡牌的星能费用预测，减少计划外重算。
- 修复忧郁、火箭飞拳在抑制解除时的费用预测错误。
- 修复虚空形态强制结束回合后，计策和暴政的回合准备选牌预测与执行错误。
- 修复音乐盒复制重压时，复制牌未按奥斯提本回合攻击记录变为零费的问题。
- 修复失衡敌人的攻击被完全格挡后，击晕意图及恢复行动的预测错误。
- 修复探寻打击的候选包含同名牌时，自动选择的卡牌与计划不一致的问题。
- 修复沿用已有路线继续计算时，药水动作违反使用策略、保护设置、配额或目标限制的问题。
- 修复采用正在计算的路线时，单轮计算结束导致路线采用失败的问题。
- 修复仪式与爬行动物饰品的临时力量同时存在时，回合末力量结算顺序错误。
- 修复毒雾配合血肉戏法时，触发伤害与敌方盾墙格挡的结算顺序错误。
- 修复死亡之门、流光溢彩等卡牌一次获得多次格挡时，不动的翻倍效果预测错误。
- 修复闪电霹雳等群体减益触发凶恶和地狱狂徒时，抽牌与自动出牌的结算顺序错误。
- 修复音乐盒复制如此甚好后，手牌接近上限时同名牌返手顺序错误。
- 修复苦难配合死神形态时，错误传播本次攻击新施加的灾厄的问题。
- 修复蜥蜴尾巴复活后，回合结束风险与路线战损比较错误。
- 修复电流相生在实际生成闪电后，重复计算生成记录导致预测充能球数量错误。
- 修复超质量体在实际生成卡牌后，重复计算生成记录导致伤害预测错误。
- 修复扯碎在实际受到伤害后，重复计算受伤次数导致攻击次数预测错误。
- 修复亡魂牵引在实际打出虚无牌后，重复计算出牌记录导致攻击次数预测错误。
- 修复金斧在实际出牌后，重复计算出牌数量导致伤害预测错误。
- 修复谋杀在实际抽牌后，重复计算抽牌数量导致伤害预测错误。
- 修复三选一页面重新打开时，自动选牌尚未被页面接受却误报成功的问题。
- 修复佩尔的士兵在一次出牌结算完成后，导致后续计算失败的问题。

## English

Changes since fork 0.48.1, incorporating upstream 0.48.1 through 0.49.0:

- Multiplayer incorporates the gold, maximum HP and Joss Paper corrections, keeping local and teammate relic ownership, death and revival states separate.
- Corrected false “better worldline” feedback after manual potion use: each additional potion must save at least 9 projected HP over the entire fight. Potions already planned by the original route are counted once.
- Reports and logs now show additional potion use and projected HP improvement after deducting potion costs. Missing potion information in older reports is marked as unknown.
- Fixed predicted draw timing when using Cascade with Unceasing Top, reducing card-selection errors and interrupted route execution.
- Fixed differences between predicted and actual end-of-turn exhaustion when Joss Paper interacts with generated cards.
- Fixed predicted hand order when Compact creates Fuel, and corrected Star-cost predictions for some cards after Bullet Time to reduce unexpected recalculations.
- Fixed cost predictions for Melancholy and Rocket Punch when Dampen is removed.
- Fixed predicted and executed turn-start card choices from Stratagem and Tyranny after Void Form forces the turn to end.
- Fixed Music Box copies of Flatten failing to become free when Osty has already attacked that turn.
- Fixed predicted Stunned intents and subsequent actions after an Imbalanced enemy's attack is fully blocked.
- Fixed Seeker Strike selecting a different card instance from the plan when its options include identical cards.
- Fixed potion actions violating usage policy, protection settings, quotas or target restrictions when continuing an existing route.
- Fixed adopting an in-progress route failing when an individual search pass finishes.
- Fixed end-of-turn Strength settlement order when Ritual and temporary Strength from Reptile Trinket are both active.
- Fixed damage from Noxious Fumes with Sleight of Flesh resolving in the wrong order relative to enemy Rampart block.
- Fixed Unmovable's block multiplier prediction when cards such as Death's Door and Glitterstream grant block multiple times in one play.
- Fixed the order of draws and automatic plays when area debuffs such as Thunderclap trigger Vicious and Hellraiser.
- Fixed return-to-hand order for identical Make It So cards copied by Music Box when the hand is near its limit.
- Fixed Misery with Reaper Form incorrectly spreading Doom newly applied by the current attack.
- Fixed end-of-turn risk and route damage-loss comparisons after revival from Lizard Tail.
- Fixed Voltaic counting Lightning-channel history again after live channeling, predicting the wrong number of orbs.
- Fixed Supermassive counting generated-card history again after live card generation, predicting incorrect damage.
- Fixed Tear Asunder counting damage events again after live damage, predicting the wrong number of hits.
- Fixed Pull from Below counting Ethereal plays again after live card plays, predicting the wrong number of hits.
- Fixed Gold Axe counting card plays again after live card plays, predicting incorrect damage.
- Fixed Murder counting draws again after live card draws, predicting incorrect damage.
- Fixed automatic card selection reporting success before the choice was accepted after a choose-a-card page reopened.
- Fixed Pael's Legion causing subsequent calculations to fail after a card play finished resolving.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed Hand of Greed rewards being evaluated using incorrect gold gains with Bowler Hat or Ectoplasm.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed missing maximum HP gains and healing from Dragon Fruit after gaining gold.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed Feed and Fruit Juice healing for more than the actual maximum HP gain near the HP cap.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed predicted Red Skull Strength changes when Fruit Juice increases maximum HP and heals.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed route evaluation overlooking post-combat maximum HP and healing opportunities from The Chosen Cheese.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed route evaluation overlooking maximum HP and healing opportunities from Darkstone Periapt when adding curses.
- [PR #203](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/203) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed preliminary calculations consuming time and causing winning routes to be missed in some time-limited searches.

Multiplayer remains manual advice only, with calculation during Toasty Mittens choices, a three-cycle local contribution goal and up to fourteen enemy cycles. Teammates' future active actions are not predicted. Route execution, automatic choices and single-player search strategy changes apply only to single-player. Live multiplayer and visible performance have not been verified.
