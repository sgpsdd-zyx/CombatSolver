# CombatSolver 0.43.5 更新日志

## 简体中文

- 合入官方 0.43.1 与 0.43.2 的更新，并汇总本 fork 自已发布的 0.43.1 以来的多人改进。
- 多人军师的规划上限从七个延长到十四个敌方周期，帮助比较高血量长战斗中的持续输出。普通多人计算使用性能设置中时间和节点上限的两倍，并为后续周期保留计算机会；默认中档最多 240 秒，可以提前结束或手动停止。详情显示实际计算额度、推演深度和选路比较范围。
- 改进多人长线选路：暂时无法击杀时，已保留的不同本回合方案都有更深推演结果，就统一比较更远周期的输出，让需要数回合回本的铺垫有机会胜出。已知死亡、救命资源和超额扣血风险继续优先；后续不完整、等待队友选择或计算余量不足时，保留原有建议。
- 修复多人遗物提示的归属，标注「自己」或「队友编号」，不同玩家同时触发同名遗物时分别显示。
- 修复多人佩尔之眼的额外回合判断：未参加队友额外回合的玩家不会被误判为可以触发；其他效果给予额外回合时，也按游戏规则消耗该玩家自己的佩尔之眼。
- 修复英文等非中文语言下悬浮窗可能无法显示的问题，药水、成长和遗物面板的“收起”按钮正确切换语言。
- 单人成长策略增加“疯狂科学”：带有“改进”效果的能力牌可独立设置战后永久升级的战损额度；其他变体不计入此项成长。多人继续按战斗内表现选择路线。
- 单人同时设置“强制使用”和“智能使用”药水时，额外智能药根据只用强制药的路线判断是否值得消耗；必要时仍可用来保命。
- 改进战斗中生成卡牌与已支持第三方卡牌效果的兼容性；遇到未支持效果时明确提示。
- 路线缓存读写失败时仍可继续计算，符合条件的战斗录像采集失败也不会阻止路线显示和执行。
- 优化单人部分整场历史计数的计算。合并时补齐多人兼容，避免多人计算开始时或使用金斧等牌时因单人假设而中断。

多人仍只给建议，需要手动请求和操作，每个敌方周期继续以本机扣血 3 HP 为目标。队友后续主动行动未纳入推演；长线收益可能以少量早期伤害为代价，不保证完成十四周期、始终安全或找到全局最优路线。真实联机与可见游戏性能尚未验收。

感谢上游维护者、bingyang1132 对 PR #105 的贡献，以及 Natsuki 对 PR #117、#118、#119 的贡献。

## English

- Incorporated upstream 0.43.1 and 0.43.2 and collected this fork's multiplayer improvements since its published 0.43.1 release.
- Extended multiplayer planning from seven to fourteen enemy cycles to compare sustained damage in long fights. Ordinary multiplayer searches use twice the time and node limits in the performance settings and reserve work for later cycles. The default Medium preset allows up to 240 seconds; searches can finish earlier or be stopped manually. Details show the effective limits, forecast depth and comparison range.
- Improved multiplayer planning when a kill is out of reach: when each retained complete current-turn plan has a deeper simulated continuation, compare their damage at the same later enemy turn. Setups that take several turns to pay off can now win that comparison. Known deaths, death-save use and excess HP loss remain priorities; incomplete continuations, required teammate choices or too little computation time keep the previous selection.
- Multiplayer relic notes now identify you or the numbered teammate who owns the relic. Identical relic effects triggered by different players remain separate.
- Corrected multiplayer Pael's Eye state: sitting out a teammate's extra turn no longer leaves the relic eligible to trigger. An extra turn granted by another effect also consumes that player's own Pael's Eye, matching the game.
- Fixed the overlay potentially failing to appear in English and other non-Chinese languages. The potion, growth, and relic panels now use the localized Collapse button.
- Added Mad Science to single-player Growth Strategy: its Power variant with Improvement has a separate HP-loss allowance for permanent deck upgrades. Other variants do not count toward this reward. Multiplayer continues to select routes by their combat outcomes.
- In single-player, when potions mix Always Use and Smart, additional Smart potions are judged against a route using only the required potions. They remain available when needed to save the fight.
- Improved compatibility for cards generated during combat and supported third-party card effects. Unsupported effects are reported explicitly.
- Route-cache read or write failures no longer prevent calculation. Failure to record an eligible combat no longer prevents the calculated route from appearing or being executed.
- Improved some whole-combat history calculations in single-player. Added multiplayer compatibility during integration to avoid interruptions at search startup or when using cards such as Gold Axe that read combat history.

Multiplayer remains manual advice with a local target of 3 HP lost per enemy cycle. Teammates' future active actions are not modeled. Long-term gains can cost early damage; completing fourteen cycles, remaining safe throughout or finding a globally optimal route is not guaranteed. Live multiplayer compatibility and visible-game performance remain unverified.

Thanks to the upstream maintainers, bingyang1132 for PR #105, and Natsuki for PRs #117, #118, and #119.
