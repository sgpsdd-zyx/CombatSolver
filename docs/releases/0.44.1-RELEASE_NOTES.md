# CombatSolver 0.44.1

## 简体中文

本版基于官方 0.44.0，以下变化以多人 fork 上一正式版 0.43.6 为对比。单人保留官方玩法与策略，多人继续手动计算和操作。

- 重新设计多人选路，取消每周期 3 HP 扣血目标。以敌人剩余血量和存活人数确定本人的阶段输出目标，同时权衡受伤、药水和后续收益；不再要求先找到独力击杀全场的路线，才考虑进攻与战损之间的取舍。
- 多人默认以三个敌方回合为一个目标阶段，继续推演最多十四个敌方回合，并考虑已找到的后续输出和能力铺垫。同阶段重新计算、治疗和额外行动回合不会重置期限；队友死亡或复活后会重新确定份额，并显示上一阶段的目标和进展。
- 多人路线摘要显示目标、已完成贡献、计划贡献及是否找到达标方案；未找到时明确提示目标未证实。队友造成的伤害不算作本人的贡献，打够份额也仍需应对真实敌人反击。
- 多人仍不预测队友未来主动出牌或用药；队友操作后可手动重算。建议仍受计算额度和已知状态限制，可能只返回较短路线，不保证十四回合安全、最大伤害或每场都优于旧版；真实联机表现仍待玩家验证。
- 单人推荐路线本回合原本就会打出“余像”时，会尝试提前它的顺序，整场受伤减少时采用；开启战后药水掉落预测后，路线摘要会显示预计掉落，满栏用药的判断只在确认能获得掉落时放宽。
- 修复部分战斗在回合开始生成卡牌、触发能力变化后计算失败的问题，以及单人智能用药插入格挡药水后，因手牌变化或提前结束战斗而计算失败的问题；调整后的顺序无法继续时保留有效路线。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #122](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/122)：修复内存回收诊断异常后，后续路线计算可能一直等待的问题，失败后仍可重新计算或手动释放内存。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #123](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/123)：单人优化部分重复出牌循环，帮助更快找到可执行击杀路线，改善过量临时格挡的干扰；修正依赖整场出牌历史和后续生成卡牌的路线判断。重复动作合并显示次数与蓝色虚线范围，隐藏的后续回合提示剩余数量，让长路线更易阅读。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #124](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/124)：单人找到满足已开启战损目标的完整路线后，更及时结束追加计算，同时保留成长、遗物、偷窃、用药和已知治疗机会的判断；调整默认搜索组合，减少重复计算并改善部分高压局面。
- 感谢 [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012) 提交的 [PR #125](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/125)：减少路线计算中的重复处理开销，保持原有路线选择规则，实际耗时改善因战斗而异。
- 战斗中生成的同名敌人按从左到右的位置区分，便于核对攻击目标和击杀提示。

## English

This release is based on upstream 0.44.0. Changes are relative to multiplayer fork 0.43.6. Single-player retains the upstream behavior and strategy; multiplayer remains manually requested advice with manual play.

- Redesigned multiplayer route selection and removed the per-cycle 3 HP loss target. Your contribution target is based on remaining enemy HP and the number of living players, balanced against health loss, potions and later returns. The solver can weigh offense against damage taken without first finding a route that defeats every enemy by itself.
- Multiplayer defaults to a three-enemy-cycle objective while still searching up to fourteen enemy cycles and considering discovered later damage and Power investments. Recalculating, healing and extra player turns do not reset the deadline. A teammate's death or revival recalculates the share and shows the previous objective and progress.
- The multiplayer summary shows your target, observed progress, planned contribution and whether a qualifying route was found. An unconfirmed target is labeled explicitly. Teammate damage does not count toward your contribution, and meeting your share does not skip actual enemy retaliation.
- Multiplayer does not predict teammates' future active card or potion use; recalculate manually after their actions. Advice remains limited by the search budget and known state and may return a short route. It does not guarantee fourteen safe turns, maximum damage or an improvement in every fight. Live multiplayer performance still needs player validation.
- In single-player, a route already playing After Image this turn may move it earlier when that reduces damage taken across the fight. With potion reward prediction enabled, the summary shows the expected drop; a full belt relaxes potion-use decisions only when a drop is confirmed and collectible.
- Fixed calculation failures when cards generated at turn start trigger Power changes, and when single-player smart potion use inserts a Block Potion that changes card states or ends combat early. A valid route is retained if the adjusted sequence cannot continue.
- [PR #122](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/122) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Fixed later calculations potentially waiting forever after a memory-reclamation diagnostic fails, allowing recalculation and manual memory release afterward.
- [PR #123](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/123) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Improved some repeating card-play loops in single-player to find executable winning routes sooner and reduced interference from excess temporary Block. Corrected route decisions involving combat-wide play history and cards generated later. Repeated actions now display a count and blue dashed range, with a remaining-turn indicator for hidden turns.
- [PR #124](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/124) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Single-player additional searches stop sooner after finding a complete route meeting the enabled HP-loss target, while retaining checks for growth, relics, stolen resources, potions and known healing opportunities. The default search combination reduces repeated work and improves some high-pressure situations.
- [PR #125](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/125) by [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012): Reduced redundant route-calculation work while preserving existing route-selection rules. The effect on calculation time varies by fight.
- Identically named enemies generated during combat are distinguished by their left-to-right positions, making targets and kill annotations easier to identify.
