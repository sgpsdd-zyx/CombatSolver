# CombatSolver 0.46.6

## 简体中文

- 修复多人模式持有烘焙手套时，回合开始选牌页面无法开始计算的问题。现在可以先查看建议耗尽的手牌与后续路线，再自行确认选择；停止计算仍可正常选牌。自己确认选择或队友改变战场后，旧建议会提示需要重新计算。

多人保持手动军师模式，不替玩家或队友操作。此选牌计算入口目前支持原版回合开始效果；第三方回合开始扩展需要先完成实际选牌。其他玩家尚在回合开始选择时，仍需等待。真实联机尚未完成验收。

## English

- Fixed multiplayer calculation being unavailable while Toasty Mittens awaits a card at the start of a turn. You can now view the suggested exhaust and subsequent route before confirming the choice yourself. Stopping calculation keeps card selection available. Confirming a choice or a teammate changing the battlefield marks the old advice as needing recalculation.

Multiplayer remains a manual advisor and does not operate any player's controls. Calculation from this choice currently supports vanilla turn-start effects; with custom turn-start extensions, finish the native choice first. Wait for other players to finish their turn-start choices. Live multiplayer validation is still pending.
