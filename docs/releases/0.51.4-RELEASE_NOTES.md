# CombatSolver 0.51.4

## 简体中文

多人 fork 同步官方 0.51.3，适配《杀戮尖塔 2》0.113.0 Beta。请同时更新 RitsuLib 至 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- 新增愚者的战斗路线计算，支持箭槽、射箭、插箭、拔箭回收、旋律组合和相关持续效果。多人军师按各玩家分别处理这些状态；额外回合与队友宠物也纳入全队预测。
- 单人支持六位角色的标准、恐惧与极速模式，包含恐惧领域、强化怪物、极速进阶及相应生成规则。单人新增「炫技」成长策略；首领战追加探索前期承伤、能力铺垫与后续击杀路线，会增加计算时间。
- 同步原有角色和通用战斗内容的新版调整，包括卡牌费用、升级、自动出牌、额外回合、随机选牌、变牌与药水生成。
- 改善附魔牌、音乐盒复制牌、谋划专家赋予奇巧、狂喜及永世沙漏加入枯萎后的路线一致性；同步乐加维林族母属性削减、仪式兽仪式、疑虑/羞耻减益持续时间和不安油灯多种减益翻倍的修正。
- 搜索详情显示整次计算的累计节点数和对应预算提示。单人自动执行遇到游戏中的出牌或药水结算异常时立即暂停，并在问题报告中保留原因。

多人继续手动请求建议与操作，只规划本机行动，不预测队友主动操作，最多推演十四个敌方周期；单人成长策略和首领追加探索不用于多人。多人仍为试用功能，尚未完成真实联机验收。恐惧与极速模式的战绩统计和精彩战斗采集尚待扩展。

## English

This multiplayer fork incorporates upstream 0.51.3 and supports Slay the Spire 2 0.113.0 Beta. Please update RitsuLib to 0.6.11 or later.

- Added combat route calculation for the Fool, including arrow slots, shooting, impaling, dislodging and retrieving arrows, melody combinations, and persistent effects. Multiplayer advice tracks these states for each player and includes extra turns and teammates' pets in party predictions.
- Single-player supports all six characters in Standard, Dread, and Blitz, including Dominions, stronger monsters, Blitz Ascension, and the corresponding generation rules. Added the Show Off growth strategy in single-player. Boss fights explore additional routes involving early HP loss, power setup, and later kills, which adds calculation time.
- Updated the existing characters and shared combat content for changes to card costs, upgrades, auto-play, extra turns, random selection, transformations, and potion generation.
- Improved route consistency for enchanted cards, Music Box copies, Sly granted by Master Planner, Ecstasy, and Wither added by Aeonglass. Incorporated fixes for Lagavulin Matriarch's stat reductions, Ceremonial Beast's Ritual, Doubt and Shame debuff duration, and Unsettling Lamp doubling multiple debuffs.
- Search details show total nodes explored across the request and the corresponding budget notices. Single-player automatic execution pauses immediately when a native card or potion action fails and preserves the cause in the issue report.

Multiplayer retains manual requests and player-controlled actions, plans only the local player's moves, does not predict teammates' active actions, and searches up to fourteen enemy cycles. Single-player growth strategies and additional boss searches do not apply to multiplayer. Multiplayer remains experimental and has not been validated in live network play. Run statistics and combat highlight collection for Dread and Blitz remain pending.
