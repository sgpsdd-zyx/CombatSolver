# 0.44.2

## 简体中文

相对多人 fork 0.44.1，本次改善多人军师对中毒等持续伤害的判断。

- 游戏未标明伤害来源的实际扣血，现在按存活人数参与选路，使持续伤害与防御之间的取舍更合理。这部分不会算成你的个人贡献；建议摘要会单独说明。
- 多人仍需手动重新计算和操作，最多推演十四个敌方周期。队友未来主动操作仍然未知，建议不保证每场都更优；真实联机表现仍待验证。单人行为保持官方 0.44.0。

## English

Compared with multiplayer fork 0.44.1, this update improves how multiplayer advice values damage over time, including Poison.

- Actual HP damage without a source identified by the game now contributes to route evaluation after division among living players, improving the tradeoff between damage over time and defense. It does not count as your personal contribution; the advice summary explains it separately.
- Multiplayer still requires manual recalculation and play, with a limit of fourteen enemy cycles. Teammates' future active actions remain unknown, and advice is not guaranteed to improve every fight. Live multiplayer behavior remains unverified. Single-player retains upstream 0.44.0 behavior.
