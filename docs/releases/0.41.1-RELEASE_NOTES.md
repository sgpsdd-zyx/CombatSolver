# CombatSolver 0.41.1 - 多人策略改进 / Multiplayer Strategy Improvements

## 简体中文

这是 `sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver` 的 fork 版本，基于官方 `0.41.0`，合并此前尚未发布的多人策略改进。

- 多人建议以每个敌方回合扣血不超过 3 HP 为目标，在此范围内优先击杀和输出，同时优先避开致命风险。已付出的扣血计入本轮额度，治疗、重新计算和额外玩家回合不会重置额度。
- 改善有限计算时间下的路线选择，让进攻、防御和先铺垫再输出的路线都有机会继续计算。
- 比较不同路线时使用共同完成的敌方回合，避免只因某条路线推演得更远就优先推荐；仍会考虑已发现的后续危险。
- 本人风险相同时优先保住队友，减少无效或过量格挡的建议。铺垫路线依据后续实际收益参与选择。
- 面板说明实际推演深度、比较边界和是否尚未完成首轮受击评估，便于判断建议的适用范围。
- 保留官方 `0.41.0` 的单人策略、能力牌改进和自动执行；上述多人策略单独生效。

多人模式只建议本人的出牌和用药，最多推演七个敌方回合。只需你安装本 Mod；你决定何时重新计算并自行操作。未来推演仍假设队友不再主动出牌或用药，队友行动后应根据新局面重新计算。

当前仍为多人试用功能：真实主机/客户端联机、三/四人组合、全角色与第三方 Mod、可见界面和普遍胜率尚未完成全面验证。

### 安装与升级

1. 使用《杀戮尖塔 2》`0.111.0`，单独安装并启用 [RitsuLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295) `0.6.0` 或更高版本。
2. 退出游戏，下载 **CombatSolver-0.41.1.zip**，将内容解压到游戏的 `mods/CombatSolver/` 目录并替换旧版；DLL 与 JSON 应直接位于该目录，无需编译。
3. 只启用一份 Combat Solver，重启游戏后进入多人战斗，等待发牌与回合开始选择完成，再点击「开始计算」。

安装包包含 Mod、Windows 辅助程序及许可文件，不包含 RitsuLib。非 Windows 系统无需运行 `CombatSolver.MemoryCleaner.exe`。

## English

This is the `sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver` fork, based on upstream `0.41.0` and including the previously unreleased multiplayer strategy improvements.

- Multiplayer advice targets at most 3 HP lost per enemy turn, favoring kills and damage within that target while prioritizing survival. HP already lost counts toward the current allowance; healing, recalculation and extra player turns do not reset it.
- Route selection under limited calculation time gives offensive, defensive and setup lines an opportunity to continue searching.
- Routes are compared at a common completed enemy turn, so searching further alone does not make a route preferable. Known danger found later still counts against that continuation.
- With equal risk to your own character, advice favors keeping teammates alive and avoids rewarding ineffective or excess Block. Setup lines are judged by the value they actually realize later.
- The panel explains the simulated depth, comparison boundary and whether the first incoming-damage cycle is still incomplete.
- Upstream `0.41.0` single-player strategy, Power improvements and automatic execution are preserved. The multiplayer changes apply separately.

Multiplayer only advises your own cards and potions, looking ahead up to seven enemy turns. Only you need to install this mod; you choose when to recalculate and perform the actions yourself. The forecast still assumes teammates take no further active card or potion actions. Recalculate from the changed battlefield after they act.

Multiplayer remains a trial feature. Live host/client networking, three/four-player parties, every character and third-party mod, the visible interface and general win-rate improvements have not undergone full validation.

### Install or Upgrade

1. Use Slay the Spire 2 `0.111.0` and separately install and enable [RitsuLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295) `0.6.0` or later.
2. Exit the game, download **CombatSolver-0.41.1.zip**, and extract its contents into the game's `mods/CombatSolver/` folder, replacing the old version. The DLL and JSON must sit directly in that folder. No compilation is needed.
3. Enable only one Combat Solver copy. Restart the game, enter multiplayer combat, finish the opening draw and turn-start choices, then click **Calculate**.

The package includes the mod, its Windows helper and license files. RitsuLib is not bundled. Other systems do not need to run `CombatSolver.MemoryCleaner.exe`.
