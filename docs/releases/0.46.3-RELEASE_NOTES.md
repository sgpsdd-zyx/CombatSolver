# CombatSolver 0.46.3 (multiplayer fork)

## 简体中文

相对多人 fork 0.45.1，本版合入官方 0.46.2，更新界面与可选启动方式，保留多人手动军师。

- 重做战斗悬浮窗、设置页、策略面板和问题包窗口：文字与控件更统一，减少重复色块、边框和装饰，深浅主题都更容易阅读。设置分组与开关状态更清楚，修复开关偶尔只显示白点的问题。
- 路线更容易逐行查看：动作类别使用简明色标，伤害、战损和能量数值排列更整齐；重复出牌的分组更紧凑，完成后随路线淡化。回合开始的选牌步骤有独立标识，中英文来源、操作及等待和完成状态更清楚。
- 感谢 [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012) 提交的 [PR #131](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/131)：求解器窗口靠近屏幕右侧时，策略侧栏可以向左展开；两侧空间都不足时靠较宽的一侧显示，减少遮挡和越界。
- 感谢 [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly) 提交的 [PR #132](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/132)：新增可选的 [ServerGC 启动方式](https://github.com/sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver/blob/v0.46.3/docs/performance/server-gc-launch-profile-20260924.md)，设置页显示本次是否生效；普通启动与保存设置保持原样。它可能降低部分战斗的内存占用或计算耗时，也可能增加 CPU 用量、变慢或改变路线战损；尚未验证本 fork 的可见流畅度或普遍收益。配套启动器用于 Windows 和 Linux，macOS 暂无配套可见启动器。
- 单人可使用在线统计和符合条件的战斗展示上传。在线统计默认开启，可在设置中关闭；字段、保留规则与关闭方式见[在线统计说明](https://github.com/sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver/blob/v0.46.3/docs/ONLINE_STATISTICS.md)。多人对局不上报。
- 多人仍由本机手动重新计算和操作，最多推演十四个敌方周期。队友独立决策，无需服从或等待建议；军师不预测其未来主动操作。建议可能改善，也可能恶化具体战斗结果，真实联机胜率与可见性能仍未验证。单人沿用官方 0.46.2 的策略与参数。

## English

Compared with multiplayer fork 0.45.1, this release incorporates upstream 0.46.2, updates the interface and optional launch settings, and retains manual multiplayer advice.

- Redesigned the combat overlay, settings, strategy panels, and bug report dialog with more consistent text and controls and fewer decorative colors and borders in both themes. Settings groups and switch states are clearer, and switches no longer occasionally appear as white dots.
- Routes are easier to read: action types use simple color markers, damage, HP loss, and energy figures line up, and repeated card play groups are more compact and dim when completed. Turn-start card choices have their own marker and clearer source, action, waiting, and completed text in Chinese and English.
- [PR #131](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/131) by [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012): Strategy sidebars can open to the left when the solver window is near the screen's right edge. When neither side has enough room, they use the roomier side to reduce overlap and overflow.
- [PR #132](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/132) by [ltlly](https://github.com/ltlly): Added an optional [ServerGC launch mode](https://github.com/sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver/blob/v0.46.3/docs/performance/server-gc-launch-profile-20260924.md), with its active status shown in settings. Normal launches and saved settings keep their existing behavior. This mode may reduce memory use or calculation time in some battles, but can increase CPU use, run slower, or change route HP loss. Visible smoothness and general benefits have not been verified for this fork. Launch scripts are provided for Windows and Linux; no matching visible launcher is provided for macOS yet.
- Single-player supports online statistics and uploads of eligible combat showcases. Online statistics are enabled by default and can be disabled in settings; see [the online statistics guide](https://github.com/sgpsdd-zyx/CombatSolver/blob/v0.46.3/docs/ONLINE_STATISTICS.md) for fields, retention, and controls. Multiplayer runs do not report statistics.
- Multiplayer still requires the local player to request recalculation and play manually, with a limit of fourteen enemy cycles. Teammates make their own decisions and need not follow or wait for advice; their future active actions are not predicted. Advice can improve or worsen individual outcomes. Live multiplayer win rates and visible performance remain unverified. Single-player retains upstream 0.46.2 strategies and settings.
